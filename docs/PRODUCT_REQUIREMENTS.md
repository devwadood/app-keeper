# Product requirements

AppLedger answers: for each Android app, developer account, and organization, how much
revenue was generated, how much was spent, and what profit or loss resulted for a date
range?

The MVP supports multiple organizations, memberships and roles, multiple Google
identities, Play profiles, Ads/AdMob accounts, canonical package identities, explicit
mapping, preliminary/final facts, manual expenses, reconciliation, exports, and source
freshness. Organization totals include unmapped facts; app totals include mapped facts
only and disclose the gap. Formal double-entry accounting and implicit FX conversion are
out of scope.

Acceptance criteria and the authoritative feature inventory live in
`CODEX_MASTER_BUILD_PROMPT.md`.
